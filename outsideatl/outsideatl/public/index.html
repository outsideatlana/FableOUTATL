[ 05 / About ]

More than an event.

OutsideAtl is an Atlanta-born events collective producing parties, festivals, concerts, raves, pop-ups, and social experiences across the city. We build rooms where the music hits harder and the crowd feels like family.

From warehouse raves to rooftop sessions, every drop is curated end-to-end — sound, space, lineup, and energy. If it's happening after hours in Atlanta, we're either throwing it or we know who is.
